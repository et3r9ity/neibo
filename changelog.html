Alpha 2.5.0 changelog

The biggest design update yet: profiles, a rebuilt report flow
and a whole new feel across the app.

July 2026 · Neibo alpha test

👤 Profiles are here

NEWYour own profile. Avatar, nickname, member-since date, your report history and live stats — reports, reactions received and views. Open it from Settings, or tap any author on a map card or post to see theirs.

NEWActivity rank. Reports and reactions you receive earn points that level you up: Newcomer → Neighbour → Local → Voice of the District → Neighbourhood Legend. Progress bar included.

NEWProfile photos. Upload an avatar straight from your gallery — it shows up in your profile.

NEWPick your name. Right after signing in (guests too), Neibo now asks what to call you — with live checks and suggestions. Nickname editing moved into your profile.

➕ Reporting — rebuilt again, properly

NEWGlass island. The report sheet is now a compact frosted-glass panel that floats up from the plus button and sizes itself to each step — no more full-screen takeover. Tap outside it to close.

NEW8 categories. Besides accidents, fires, utilities and city life there are two fresh ones: Vibe (sunsets, cool cars, street art) and Spots (finds, cute animals, new places). Because the alpha showed you post good moments, not just emergencies.

NEWMorphing categories. Pick a category and it glides to the centre while subcategories appear around it as neat tiles. Tap it again to bring the full grid back.

NEWCleaner location step. Just the map: your position, a centered pin and a visible 500 m circle showing exactly how far you can place the marker. Too far? The button simply greys out.

NEWSwipe to go back. iOS-style edge swipe steps you back through the wizard; a segmented progress bar and a back button live in the header.

NEWPhoto & details, minimal. A big photo square and a borderless notes field — nothing extra.

❤️ Reactions, reimagined

NEWHeart first. Every event card has a quick heart — tap to like. An arrow next to it unfolds the full row: like, dislike, laugh, cry and a clown we drew ourselves.

NEWIcons, not emojis. Reactions are now clean minimal icons that match the app, with per-reaction counters. Your pick stays highlighted in its own colour.

🗺️ Map, alive

NEWLive pulse. Ongoing events breathe with a red halo right on the map — you can spot what's happening now at a glance.

NEWCleaner markers. No more pointy tails — just crisp circles that pop in smoothly. Clusters form less eagerly and grow in gently.

NEWSmoother cards. Tapping a marker opens the event card with a quick soft bounce. Swipe down to dismiss. Confirm buttons and crosses are gone — the app asks eyewitnesses itself, Waze-style.

NEWRadius, back where it belongs. The radius control returned to the "Nearby" sheet — tap the radar icon to adjust with a slider and presets.

🧭 New look & feel

NEWFloating dock. Navigation is now a frosted-glass pill hovering above the content, icon-only, with a soft sliding highlight and icons that fill in when active.

NEWWarmer design language. New palette, softer shadows, gentle spring physics and subtler haptics across the whole app — vibration only where it means something.

NEWWelcome screen redesign. Cleaner start: one-tap guest entry or email sign-in on a tidy bottom panel.

NEWSettings, decluttered. A profile card on top, notifications moved to their own page, and your nickname now lives in the profile.

🛡️ Keeping the map trustworthy

NEWAI moderation. Reports with offensive text or inappropriate photos are checked automatically before they reach the map.

NEWView counters. Events count unique views — see how many neighbours noticed a report.

NEWStronger moderation tools. The team can now ban accounts that abuse the map — banned users can't post or react.

🔧 Fixed along the way

FIXMarkers sometimes ignored the first tap on iOS — not anymore.

FIXThe report sheet could open stuck to the top of the screen after using the keyboard.

FIXThe radius button occasionally went dead until app restart.

FIXFullscreen photos no longer wobble — and they grow out of the exact frame you tapped.

FIXDozens of small animation, layering and layout fixes across map, feed and report.

⚠️ Known issues

Push notifications are still off in the alpha build — the notifications page is a preview. Coming with a future release.

Auto-parsed news don't have exact map locations yet — we're working on it.
