Alpha 4.1.0 changelog

Neibo in Latvian, a clear choice about AI before your first photo, reports on comments — and a move to the new Expo Go.

1 November 2026 · Neibo alpha test

⬆️ Update Expo Go first

This release runs on Expo SDK 58. Update Expo Go from the App Store or Google Play, then open Neibo again. If you see «Project is incompatible with this version of Expo Go», your Expo Go is still the old one. On iPhone, the new Expo Go could not open 4.0.1 at all — 4.1.0 fixes that.

🇱🇻 Neibo in Latvian

NEWThe whole interface in Latvian. Screens, buttons, categories and types — everything is translated. Choose Latviešu or Русский in Settings → Language; the app remembers your choice.

NEWRight language from the start. On first launch Neibo follows your phone's language, and uses Latvian when your phone is set to anything else.

🤖 You decide about AI

NEWA clear question before the first photo. Before your first snap, Neibo explains what goes to Google Gemini to fill in the report and what OpenAI checks before anything is published. Allow it, or choose Without recognition and fill in the fields yourself.

NEWChange your mind any time. The Photo recognition switch is the first one under Publishing in Settings.

🚩 Safer comments

NEWReport or block from a comment. Press and hold someone else's comment: report it with a reason, or block the person.

📄 Rules and help

NEWPrivacy Policy and Terms of Use are now in the app instead of a placeholder. They are still drafts and say so at the top.

NEWWrite to support — the first line in About Neibo. The app version is added to the message for you.

FIXNo more «coming soon». The unfinished «Categories» and «My territories» previews are hidden from Alerts until notifications can reach your phone.

🔧 Fixed along the way

FIXOn a slow start the app could replace your saved account with a new guest. It now waits for your session first.

FIX«Delete account» works. It removes your account and data on the server; what is kept, and for how long, is in the Privacy Policy.

🛡️ Under the hood

Photos open through short-lived links instead of permanent public addresses.

A post can only use your own photo, and a profile picture can only come from Neibo's own storage.

⚠️ Known issues

Reporting a comment may show an error for a few days. The app part is ready; the server part is being switched on.

AI descriptions are still written in Russian, even with the Latvian interface. Latvian descriptions come next.

Sign in with Apple or Google is not switched on yet. Email and guest accounts work.

Push notifications still don't arrive in Expo Go. Alerts work inside the app; system notifications need a standalone build.
