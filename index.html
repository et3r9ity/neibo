Join the Neibo alpha

Live map of Latvia: accidents, fires, outages and city events — everything happening nearby, in one app.

1

Install Expo Go — the alpha runs inside it.

App Store Google Play
2

Create a free account at expo.dev

3

Send your expo.dev email to our Telegram channel @neibo_app (tap Message) — we'll invite you

4

Accept the invite — check your inbox for an email from Expo ("join rigamap")

5

Log in to Expo Go with your account, then tap the button below

Open Neibo in Expo Go

The button works after steps 1–5. Updates arrive automatically — just reopen the app.

Already in the alpha? Alpha 3.0.0 runs on a new Expo runtime — update Expo Go from the App Store or Google Play first, otherwise you will keep opening the old version. See what's new

Seeing "Project is incompatible with this version of Expo Go"? Your Expo Go is older than the app needs — update it in the store, then tap the button again.

Seeing "Something went wrong"? You haven't accepted the invite yet, or Expo Go is logged into a different account. News & patch notes: t.me/neibo_app
